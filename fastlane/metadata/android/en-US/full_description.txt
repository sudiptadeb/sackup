SackUp backs up your phone files (photos, videos, documents, music) to a USB drive via OTG cable. No cloud services, no accounts, no internet required.

It comes with three backup groups ready to go — Photos (DCIM and Pictures), Documents and Music — and you can add a group for any folder you choose.

Features:
* One-tap backup with large buttons and simple words, designed for everyone
* Only copies what is new — files already on the drive are skipped
* Every copied file is written all the way to the drive and checked before it counts as saved
* Check what is backed up, even without the drive plugged in
* Free Up Space: safely remove files from the phone that are confirmed on the drive
* Keeps going in the background with a progress notification
* Works completely offline — your files never leave your devices
* Free and open source
